Three bands, then nine cells

Both authoring experiments have been executed on the front and back, using the same raw generated clothing and fixed body visibility masks. The side composition was copied unchanged. No ImageGen call, game launch or native validation was performed.

Result: fitting remains unresolved. Three bands correct cuff height while holding the lower robe fixed. Nine cells additionally control the two sleeve widths separately. Neither automatically resolves the mismatch between the actual cuff edge, cloth overlap and the older painted visibility selection. These are comparative drafts, not accepted garments.

MethodWhat actually changes
Three bandsLandmark-based shoulder/cuff/transition bands, with shared boundaries. Upper width follows arm-center separation; middle joins the fixed lower section.
Nine cellsThe same three rows plus left sleeve, torso and right sleeve columns. Cuff-row column positions follow the two actual arm rectangles independently.
FrozenBody, mask and lower common-baseline pixels remain fixed. The accepted profile image is byte-for-byte unchanged.

One continuous coordinate map samples the original complete cloth per method. Before fitting, exterior hand-occlusion notches were bridged between visible cloth edges and missing fabric continued from that same image. Those concealed surfaces are authored reconstruction, not recovered detail. No cutoff-derived alpha is reused.
