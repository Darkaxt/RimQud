80 × 25 cells · 996 authored placements · 1,810 mapped cells · 190 original fallback cells

Ground, paths, grass, crops, reeds, trees, doors, water and the main brinestalk walls use donor materials. The capture has 274 grass clumps at Qud's native paint locations and 51 connected shallow-water cells. NPCs, furniture and unmapped structures keep Qud's sprites; native foregrounds over mapped water expose the liquid underneath.

Path interiors and their eight-neighbor transitions share the same donor material, texture sampling and terrain precedence. Shallow/deep appearance follows Qud's actual volume thresholds; all baseline ponds are wading depth. The liquid adapter uses donor ramp textures, not RimWorld's complete animated water pipeline.

This controlled simulation loads Joppa.rpm through Qud's map builder over an explicit dirt-floor fill. It is not a fully generated world or a normal character session. Gameplay controls, targeting and save/load remain unverified.
